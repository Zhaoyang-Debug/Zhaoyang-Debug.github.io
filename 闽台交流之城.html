MODULE 1 · SHARED ROOTS

唐山过台湾——跨越海峡的迁徙史

"Tangshan to Taiwan": A Migration History Across the Strait

从明末清初到改革开放，泉州人用四次大规模迁徙在海峡两岸织就了血脉相连的网络。

From the late Ming dynasty to reform and opening-up, Quanzhou people wove a blood-connected network across the strait through four major migrations.

明末清初 · 17世纪
Late Ming - Early Qing · 17th Century

郑成功收复台湾

Koxinga Recovers Taiwan

1662年，泉州南安人郑成功驱逐荷兰殖民者，收复台湾。郑成功收复台湾后设府建置、推行屯田，大批泉州军民随之入台开垦，台湾由此迎来大规模开发。郑成功被两岸同胞共同尊为民族英雄。

In 1662, Koxinga (Zheng Chenggong), a native of Quanzhou's Nan'an, expelled Dutch colonists and recovered Taiwan. Subsequently, large numbers of Quanzhou soldiers and civilians settled in Taiwan, clearing land and building homes—forming Taiwan's earliest Han Chinese communities. Koxinga is revered as a national hero on both sides of the strait.

清代中期 · 18世纪
Mid-Qing · 18th Century

大规模移民潮

Large-Scale Migration Wave

清康熙二十二年（1683年）统一台湾，次年设台湾府，隶属福建省。泉州人掀起了大规模移民台湾的浪潮，渡海到台湾西部平原开垦，将闽南的农耕技术、宗族制度、民间信仰带到台湾。嘉义东石、鹿港、笨港（今北港）多由泉州移民以故土之名命名、聚族开发。

After the Qing dynasty unified Taiwan in the Kangxi era, Quanzhou people launched a large-scale migration wave. They crossed the strait to clear Taiwan's western plains, bringing Minnan farming techniques, clan systems, and folk beliefs. Many Taiwanese place names (such as Lukang and Bengang) originated from Quanzhou immigrants.

日据时期 · 1895-1945
Japanese Occupation · 1895-1945

文化坚守

Cultural Perseverance

甲午战争后台湾被割让日本，日本推行"皇民化"运动，试图消灭汉语和中国文化。但台湾同胞坚持在家中说闽南语、拜妈祖、过春节，用文化坚守维系着与大陆的血脉联系。泉州与台湾的民间往来从未完全中断。

After the First Sino-Japanese War, Taiwan was ceded to Japan, which launched a "Japanization" movement attempting to eliminate Chinese language and culture. But Taiwanese people insisted on speaking Minnan at home, worshipping Mazu, and celebrating Spring Festival—maintaining blood ties with the mainland through cultural perseverance. Folk exchanges between Quanzhou and Taiwan never fully ceased.

改革开放后 · 1987至今
Post-Reform · 1987-Present

交流重启

Exchanges Resume

1987年，台湾当局开放台湾同胞赴大陆探亲，两岸长达38年的隔绝状态被打破，交流由此重启。泉州凭借地缘、血缘、文缘优势，成为台商投资大陆的重要聚集地和两岸文化交流的前沿阵地。今天，泉州有数千家台资企业，每年有大量台湾同胞来泉州寻根谒祖、旅游交流。

In 1987, Taiwan opened up for veterans to visit mainland relatives, restarting cross-strait exchanges. With its geographic, blood, and cultural advantages, Quanzhou became a major destination for Taiwanese investment and a frontier of cross-strait cultural exchange. Today, Quanzhou hosts thousands of Taiwanese-funded enterprises, with numerous Taiwanese compatriots visiting yearly for ancestral roots, tourism, and exchange.

MODULE 2 · SHARED CULTURE

一样的语言，一样的信仰

Same Language, Same Faith

语言、信仰、宗族、习俗——四个维度，见证闽台两地文化的同根同源。

Language, faith, clan, customs—four dimensions witnessing the deep cultural isomorphism between Minnan and Taiwan.

语言相通

Shared Language

台湾主流方言是闽南语，与泉州话同属闽南语系，语音、词汇、语法高度一致。台湾的"国语"中也融入了大量闽南语词汇。泉州人到台湾，用闽南语可以无障碍交流。语言是文化的载体，共同的语言承载着共同的文化记忆。

Taiwan's mainstream dialect is Minnan (Hokkien), belonging to the same language family as Quanzhou dialect with highly consistent pronunciation, vocabulary, and grammar. Taiwanese "Mandarin" also incorporates many Minnan words. Quanzhou people can communicate freely in Taiwan using Minnan. Language is the carrier of culture—shared language carries shared cultural memory.

信仰相同

Shared Faith

妈祖信仰是闽台两地最具代表性的民间信仰。泉州天后宫是世界上规模最大的妈祖庙之一。台湾有3000余座妈祖庙，每年都有大量台湾信众到泉州天后宫、湄洲妈祖祖庙进香。妈祖文化是两岸同胞共同的精神纽带。

Mazu worship is the most representative folk belief on both sides. Quanzhou's Tianhou Temple is one of the world's largest Mazu temples. Taiwan has over 500 Mazu temples, and every year large numbers of Taiwanese believers make pilgrimages to Quanzhou's Tianhou Temple and Meizhou Mazu Ancestral Temple. Mazu culture is a shared spiritual bond across the strait.

Note: Baosheng Dadi, Guan Yu, and Qingshui Zushi are also jointly worshipped in Minnan and Taiwan.

宗族同根

Clan Kinship

台湾汉族同胞中，约44.8%祖籍泉州。台湾的许多姓氏（如陈、林、黄、张、王）都可以追溯到泉州的同宗同族。台湾各姓宗亲会经常组团到泉州寻根谒祖，修缮祖祠、续修族谱。宗族血脉是两岸最牢固的联系之一。

Approximately 44.8% of Taiwan's Han Chinese trace their ancestry to Quanzhou. Many Taiwanese surnames (Chen, Lin, Huang, Zhang, Wang) can be traced to Quanzhou clans. Taiwanese clan associations regularly organize groups to visit Quanzhou for ancestral worship, renovating ancestral halls and updating genealogy books. Clan bloodlines are among the strongest cross-strait bonds.

习俗同源

Shared Customs

闽台两地共享相同的民俗传统——元宵赏花灯、端午赛龙舟、中秋博饼、冬至搓圆。闽南的婚丧嫁娶、建筑风格、饮食文化（面线糊、土笋冻、姜母鸭）在台湾都有传承。这些日常习俗，是两岸同根同源最生动的证明。

Minnan and Taiwan share the same folk traditions—Lantern Festival lanterns, Dragon Boat Festival races, Mid-Autumn mooncake gambling (bo-bing), and Winter Solstice rice balls. Minnan wedding and funeral customs, architectural styles, and food culture (misua paste, sandworm jelly, ginger duck) are all preserved in Taiwan. These daily customs are the most vivid proof of shared roots.

MODULE 3 · AUTHORITATIVE WITNESS

闽台缘博物馆——用文物讲述两岸一家亲

China Museum for Fujian-Taiwan Kinship: Telling "One Family" Through Artifacts

中国闽台缘博物馆坐落于泉州，是全国唯一的国家级对台专题博物馆、国家一级博物馆，2006年5月建成开放，约四成藏品由台胞捐赠。博物馆以"闽台缘"为主题，通过大量文物、文献、图片，系统展示了闽台两地在地理、血缘、法缘、商缘、文缘等方面的密切关系，是两岸同根同源的权威见证。

Located in Quanzhou, the China Museum for Fujian-Taiwan Kinship is mainland China's only national-level museum dedicated to Fujian-Taiwan relations and their historical and cultural origins. With the theme of "Fujian-Taiwan kinship," it systematically displays the close geographic, blood, legal, commercial, and cultural ties through artifacts, documents, and photographs—an authoritative witness to shared cross-strait roots.

EXHIBIT 01

远古家园

Ancient Homeland — Geographic Proximity

台湾海峡存在一定的地质变迁。远古时期，台湾与大陆相连，后来地壳运动形成海峡。考古发现证明，台湾史前文化与大陆东南沿海文化一脉相承。

Displays the geological evolution of the Taiwan Strait. In ancient times, Taiwan was connected to the mainland; later crustal movement formed the strait. Archaeological finds prove Taiwan's prehistoric culture is continuous with mainland China's southeast coast culture.

EXHIBIT 02

血缘同源

Blood Kinship — Shared Ancestry

明清时期泉州人移民台湾具有悠久的历史，馆内包括族谱、渡海工具、开垦契约等文物。用数据展示台湾汉族同胞的祖籍分布，其中泉州籍占比最高。

Displays the history of Quanzhou migration to Taiwan during the Ming-Qing period, including genealogy books, sea-crossing tools, and land reclamation contracts. Data shows the ancestral distribution of Taiwan's Han Chinese, with Quanzhou origins accounting for the largest share.

EXHIBIT 03

法缘相循

Legal Continuity — Historical Evolution

台湾从三国时期到清代经历了多次历史沿革，用历代官方文献、舆图证明台湾自古以来就是中国领土不可分割的一部分。

Displays Taiwan's historical evolution from the Three Kingdoms period to the Qing dynasty, using official documents and maps to prove Taiwan has been an inalienable part of Chinese territory since ancient times.

EXHIBIT 04

商缘相连

Commercial Ties — Economic Exchange

闽台两地从古代“郊行”贸易到当代台商投资的经贸往来历史悠久。闽台两地同名同宗村约280对，仅与泉州同名的台湾地名就有180多处；晋江东石与嘉义东石隔海同名，两岸东石人同拜九龙三公、共数元宵宫灯，正是闽台移民与商贸同源的生动见证。

Displays the history of Fujian-Taiwan economic exchange from ancient "jiaohang" trade to contemporary Taiwanese investment. Quanzhou's Lugang and Taiwan's Lukang (Changhua) share the same name due to trade—a historical witness to cross-strait commerce.

EXHIBIT 05

文缘相承

Cultural Continuity — Shared Heritage

闽台具有共同的语言、信仰、戏曲、民俗、建筑、饮食文化。南音、歌仔戏、木偶戏在两岸共同传承，妈祖、保生大帝信仰在两岸共同信奉。

Displays shared Minnan-Taiwan language, faith, opera, folklore, architecture, and food culture. Nanyin, Gezai opera, and puppet theatre are jointly inherited; Mazu and Baosheng Dadi are jointly worshipped on both sides.

MUSEUM

博物馆建筑

The Museum Building

博物馆主体建筑设计融合了闽南传统建筑元素与现代风格，"出砖入石"墙体、燕尾脊等符号彰显闽台同根的建筑文化。馆区占地面积100余亩，建筑面积2万余平方米。

The museum's architecture blends traditional Minnan elements with modern style—"brick-and-stone" walls and swallow-tail roofs symbolize shared building culture. The site covers over 100 mu with 20,000+ sqm of floor space.

MODULE 4 · INTEGRATED DEVELOPMENT

融合发展——新时代的两岸故事

Integrated Development: Cross-Strait Stories in a New Era

台商投资、青年交流、文化节庆——三个案例，呈现新时代两岸融合发展的生动实践。

Taiwanese investment, youth exchange, cultural festivals—three cases presenting the vivid practice of cross-strait integrated development in the new era.

经济融合 ECONOMIC INTEGRATION

台商在泉州

Taiwanese Businesspeople in Quanzhou

泉州是台商投资大陆的重要聚集地。截至目前，泉州累计批准台资企业数千家，投资领域涵盖机械、电子、食品、纺织、农业等。许多台商在泉州扎根多年，把泉州当成"第二故乡"。泉州台商投资区是国家级台商投资区，为台胞提供创业就业的优质平台。

Quanzhou is a major destination for Taiwanese investment, with thousands of approved Taiwanese-funded enterprises in machinery, electronics, food, textiles, and agriculture. Many Taiwanese businesspeople have rooted in Quanzhou for years, calling it their "second hometown." The Quanzhou Taiwanese Investment Zone is a national-level zone providing a high-quality platform for Taiwanese entrepreneurship and employment.

青年交流 YOUTH EXCHANGE

两岸青年交流

Cross-Strait Youth Exchange

每年，大量台湾青年学生到泉州实习、创业、交流。泉州的高校与台湾高校建立了合作关系，举办两岸大学生夏令营、创业大赛。台湾青年在泉州体验闽南文化、了解大陆发展，许多人选择留在泉州就业创业。

Every year, large numbers of Taiwanese youth come to Quanzhou for internships, entrepreneurship, and exchange. Quanzhou universities partner with Taiwanese universities, hosting cross-strait student summer camps and startup competitions. Taiwanese youth experience Minnan culture and learn about mainland development, with many choosing to stay and work in Quanzhou.

民生相融 CULTURAL EXCHANGE

共饮一江水——泉州向金门供水

Mazu Cultural Tourism Festival

2018年8月5日，福建向金门供水工程正式通水。工程自泉州晋江龙湖取水，经陆地与跨海管道送至金门，线路总长约28公里，设计日供水3.4万立方米。这一工程历经二十余载磋商，终让金门同胞喝上泉州水；截至2026年，累计供水已突破5000万吨，约占金门民生供水的七成。"两岸一家亲、共饮一江水"，由此从愿景照进现实。

Every year, Quanzhou hosts the Mazu Cultural Tourism Festival, attracting large numbers of Taiwanese believers and tourists. Cross-strait Mazu temples hold joint worship ceremonies, with Taiwanese Mazu temples conducting "incense division" rituals at Quanzhou's Tianhou Temple—an important activity of cross-strait religious and cultural exchange.
