01 / ADMIN

九日山祈风石刻

Jiuri Mountain Inscriptions

行政管理 Administration
南宋（12-13世纪）
Southern Song (12th-13th C.)

每年冬夏季风转换时节，泉州市舶司官员率商民在九日山举行祈风仪式，祈求航海顺风。现存10方宋代祈风石刻，是中国古代海外贸易管理的珍贵实物见证。

During the Song dynasty, Maritime Trade Office officials held wind-praying ceremonies at Jiuri Mountain. Ten surviving Song inscriptions are precious evidence of ancient China's maritime trade administration.

02 / ADMIN

市舶司遗址

Maritime Trade Office

行政管理 Administration
北宋（1087年设立）
Northern Song (est. 1087)

北宋元祐二年（1087年），朝廷在泉州设立市舶司，管理海外贸易、征收关税、接待外商。这是泉州成为官方对外贸易港口的标志。

Established in 1087, the Maritime Trade Office managed overseas trade, collected tariffs, and received foreign merchants—marking Quanzhou's status as an official trade port.

03 / ADMIN

南外宗正司遗址

Southern Clan Court

行政管理 Administration
南宋
Southern Song

南宋皇室宗族管理机构，管理迁居泉州的赵氏宗室。大量皇族聚居泉州，带动了城市的繁荣和文化的发展。

The Southern Song imperial clan administration, managing the Zhao imperial family members who relocated to Quanzhou, driving the city's prosperity and cultural development.

04 / TRADE

德济门遗址

Deji Gate Site

商贸设施 Trade Facility
南宋至明代
Southern Song-Ming

宋元泉州城南城门，门外是阿拉伯、波斯商人聚居的"蕃坊"。出土了大量外来宗教石刻和陶瓷，见证了多元文化生态。

The south gate of Song-Yuan Quanzhou, leading to the "foreign quarter" of Arab and Persian merchants. Excavations uncovered foreign religious carvings and ceramics.

05 / TRADE

德化窑址

Dehua Kiln Sites

商贸设施 Trade Facility
宋元至明清
Song-Yuan to Ming-Qing

宋元时期泉州外销瓷的主要产地，以白瓷闻名。产品经泉州港远销海外，在东亚、东南亚、西亚、东非均有出土。

The main production base for export porcelain in Song-Yuan Quanzhou, famous for white porcelain. Products were exported worldwide and excavated across multiple regions.

06 / TRADE

安溪青阳冶铁遗址

Anxi Iron Smelting Site

商贸设施 Trade Facility
宋元时期
Song-Yuan Period

宋元时期泉州冶铁手工业的重要遗址，出土大量炉渣、铁制品和冶炼设施。泉州的铁制品经海上丝绸之路外销。

An important Song-Yuan iron smelting site, yielding slag, iron products, and smelting facilities. Quanzhou's iron goods were exported via the Maritime Silk Road.

07 / TRADE

磁灶窑址

Cizao Kiln Sites

商贸设施 Trade Facility
宋元时期
Song-Yuan Period

泉州南郊的民间窑场，以酱釉、绿釉瓷器闻名，产品主要外销东南亚，是泉州外销瓷的重要组成部分。

Folk kilns in southern Quanzhou, famous for brown and green glazed ceramics, mainly exported to Southeast Asia—an important part of Quanzhou's export porcelain.

08 / RELIGION

开元寺

Kaiyuan Temple

文化地标 Cultural Landmark
唐代始建（686年）
Founded Tang (686)

始建于唐垂拱二年，寺内东西塔建于南宋，高40余米，是中国现存最高的一对石塔。大雄宝殿斗拱雕有24尊飞天乐伎。

Founded in 686, its East and West Pagodas stand over 40m tall—the tallest surviving stone pagodas in China. The Main Hall features 24 flying apsara musicians.

09 / RELIGION

清净寺

Qingjing Mosque

宗教建筑 Religious Site
北宋（1009年）
Northern Song (1009)

中国现存最古老的伊斯兰教寺，由阿拉伯商人始建于1009年。它是泉州作为国际商埠、多元文化交融的实物见证。

China's oldest surviving mosque, founded by Arab merchants in 1009. It is physical evidence of Quanzhou's role as an international trading port and multicultural hub.

10 / RELIGION

草庵摩尼光佛造像

Cao'an Manichaean Buddha

宗教建筑 Religious Site
元代（1339年）
Yuan Dynasty (1339)

世界仅存的摩尼教石雕造像，雕刻于元代。摩尼教在唐代传入中国，在泉州与佛教、道教融合，是宗教交流的独特见证。

The world's only surviving Manichaean stone Buddha statue, carved in the Yuan dynasty. Manichaeism fused with Buddhism and Daoism in Quanzhou—a unique witness to religious exchange.

11 / RELIGION

天后宫

Tianhou Temple

宗教建筑 Religious Site
南宋（1196年）
Southern Song (1196)

祭祀海神妈祖的庙宇，是海内外建筑规格最高、规模最大的妈祖庙之一。妈祖信仰随泉州商人传播到世界各地。

A temple dedicated to sea goddess Mazu, one of the highest-standard and largest Mazu temples worldwide. Mazu worship spread globally with Quanzhou merchants.

12 / RELIGION

真武庙

Zhenwu Temple

宗教建筑 Religious Site
宋代
Song Dynasty

祭祀海神真武大帝的庙宇，是宋元时期泉州商人出海前祭拜海神、祈求平安的重要场所。

A temple dedicated to sea god Zhenwu, where Quanzhou merchants prayed for safe voyages before setting sail during the Song-Yuan period.

13 / RELIGION

伊斯兰教圣墓

Islamic Holy Tombs

宗教建筑 Religious Site
唐代
Tang Dynasty

唐代来华的伊斯兰教三贤、四贤的墓葬，是伊斯兰教传入中国的重要史迹，见证了中阿文化交流的悠久历史。

Tombs of two early Islamic missionaries who came to China in the Tang dynasty—important historical evidence of Islam's introduction to China and Sino-Arab cultural exchange.

14 / CULTURE

泉州府文庙

Quanzhou Confucian Temple

文化地标 Cultural Landmark
唐代始建
Founded Tang Dynasty

中国东南地区规模最大的文庙建筑群，是泉州重教兴学传统的象征。宋元时期泉州科举兴盛，人才辈出。

The largest Confucian temple complex in southeast China, symbolizing Quanzhou's tradition of valuing education. The Song-Yuan period saw flourishing imperial examinations here.

15 / CULTURE

老君岩造像

Laozi Rock Statue

文化地标 Cultural Landmark
宋代
Song Dynasty

中国现存最大的宋代道教石雕老君像，高5.63米。造像慈祥端庄，是泉州道教文化的标志性遗存。

China's largest surviving Song-dynasty Taoist stone statue of Laozi, standing 5.63m tall. The serene figure is an iconic remnant of Quanzhou's Taoist culture.

16 / TRANSPORT

洛阳桥

Luoyang Bridge

交通枢纽 Transport Hub
北宋（1053-1059年）
Northern Song (1053-1059)

由北宋泉州太守蔡襄主持修建，首创"筏型基础"和"种蛎固基"技术，是中国现存最早的跨海梁式石桥。

Built under prefect Cai Xiang, pioneering "raft foundation" and "oyster-bed consolidation" techniques—China's earliest surviving cross-sea stone beam bridge.

17 / TRANSPORT

安平桥

Anping Bridge

交通枢纽 Transport Hub
南宋（1138年）
Southern Song (1138)

全长2255米，是中古时期世界上最长的梁式石桥，有"天下无桥长此桥"之誉。是泉州内陆与沿海商贸的重要通道。

At 2,255m, the world's longest medieval beam-style stone bridge, praised as "no bridge under heaven is longer." A vital commerce channel between Quanzhou's interior and coast.

18 / TRANSPORT

顺济桥遗址

Shunji Bridge Site

交通枢纽 Transport Hub
南宋
Southern Song

南宋时期建造的石桥，连接泉州城与晋江流域，是泉州古城向南拓展的重要交通设施。

A Southern Song stone bridge connecting Quanzhou city to the Jinjiang River basin—an important transport facility for the ancient city's southward expansion.

19 / TRANSPORT

江口码头

Jiangkou Dock

交通枢纽 Transport Hub
宋元时期
Song-Yuan Period

泉州古城内港码头，是商船装卸货物、商人登岸的重要场所，见证了泉州港的繁华。

An inner harbor dock in ancient Quanzhou, where merchant ships loaded/unloaded cargo and merchants came ashore—witnessing the port's prosperity.

20 / TRANSPORT

石湖码头

Shihu Dock

交通枢纽 Transport Hub
唐宋时期
Tang-Song Period

泉州湾外港码头，是远洋商船停泊和补给的重要基地，也是泉州港通往世界的门户。

An outer harbor dock in Quanzhou Bay, an important base for ocean-going ships to anchor and resupply—the gateway from Quanzhou Port to the world.

21 / TRANSPORT

六胜塔

Liusheng Pagoda

交通枢纽 Transport Hub
元代
Yuan Dynasty

泉州湾入海处的航标塔，为进出泉州港的商船指引方向。是古代海上丝绸之路的重要导航设施。

A navigation beacon tower at the entrance of Quanzhou Bay, guiding merchant ships in and out of the port—an important navigation facility of the ancient Maritime Silk Road.

22 / TRANSPORT

万寿塔

Wanshou Pagoda

交通枢纽 Transport Hub
宋代
Song Dynasty

又称"关锁塔"，矗立在泉州湾南岸，是商船出入泉州港的重要航标，也是泉州港的标志性建筑。

Also called "Guansuo Pagoda," standing on Quanzhou Bay's south shore—an important navigation beacon for merchant ships and an iconic landmark of Quanzhou Port.

Living Heritage · A Three-Tier Map from World to Provincial

不在玻璃柜里，在日常生活中

Not in Glass Cases, but in Daily Life

泉州是全国唯一拥有联合国教科文组织三大类非遗项目的城市（代表作名录、急需保护名录、优秀实践名册）

Quanzhou is China's only city with UNESCO intangible heritage in all three categories (Representative List, Urgent Safeguarding List, Register of Good Practices)

传统音乐 · 2009年 · 代表作名录
Traditional Music · 2009 · Representative List

南音

Nanyin

被誉为“中国音乐史上的活化石”，有千余年历史，以琵琶、洞箫、二弦、三弦为主要乐器，保留了唐宋古乐遗韵。至今仍在宗祠、茶馆、社区定期演出。

A "living fossil of Chinese music history" with over a millennium of history. Featuring pipa, dongxiao, erxian, and sanxian, it preserves Tang-Song musical echoes and still performs regularly in communities.

传统技艺 · 2009年 · 代表作名录
Traditional Craft · 2009 · Representative List

闽南民居营造技艺

Minnan Architecture

中国传统木结构营造技艺的重要组成部分。闽南红砖厝以"出砖入石"墙体、燕尾脊屋顶、雕梁画栋为特色，是中国传统建筑的独特流派。

A key part of traditional Chinese wooden architecture. Minnan red-brick houses feature "brick-and-stone" walls, swallow-tail roofs, and ornate carvings—a unique architectural school.

传统技艺 · 2010年 · 急需保护名录
Traditional Craft · 2010 · Urgent Safeguarding

水密隔舱福船制造技艺

Watertight Bulkhead Shipbuilding

古代中国造船技术的伟大发明，将船舱分隔为多个水密隔舱，大大提升了航海安全性。这一技术后来传播到世界各地，影响了全球造船业。

A great ancient Chinese shipbuilding invention, dividing hulls into watertight compartments to vastly improve maritime safety. This technology spread worldwide and influenced global shipbuilding.

传统戏剧 · 2012年 · 优秀实践名册
Traditional Theatre · 2012 · Good Practices

福建木偶戏后继人才培养计划

Fujian Puppet Talent Development

泉州提线木偶戏有两千余年历史，一个木偶最多36条提线。该项目以系统化的传承人培养模式入选优秀实践名册，为全球非遗传承提供了中国经验。

Quanzhou marionette theatre has 2,000+ years of history, with up to 36 strings per puppet. Its systematic inheritor training model was inscribed on the Register of Good Practices, offering Chinese experience to global heritage transmission.

民俗 · 2020年 · 代表作名录
Folklore · 2020 · Representative List

送王船

Sending the Wangchuan

有关人与海洋可持续联系的仪式及相关实践。泉州沿海渔村定期建造并焚烧王船，送代天巡狩的王爷回天庭，祈求风调雨顺、航海平安，是海洋文化的活态传承。

A ritual of sustainable human-ocean relations. Coastal villages build and burn "Wangchuan" (king ships) to send deities back to heaven, praying for fair weather and safe voyages—a living maritime heritage.

民俗 · 2024年 · 代表作名录
Folklore · 2024 · Representative List

春节

Spring Festival

中国人庆祝传统新年的社会实践。泉州的春节习俗保留了浓厚的闽南特色——拜天公、博饼、逛花灯、吃润饼，是中华年俗文化的活态传承。

The social practice of celebrating the Chinese New Year. Quanzhou's Spring Festival retains strong Minnan features—worshipping heaven, mooncake dice games, lantern fairs, and eating runbing flatbreads.

中国传统制茶技艺及其相关习俗

Chinese Traditional Tea Processing and Related Practices

泉州多地的制茶技艺共同构成中国传统制茶技艺体系。除安溪铁观音外，永春佛手、南安石亭绿等也各具特色，共同见证了中国茶文化的丰富多样性。

Multiple Quanzhou tea-processing techniques form part of China's traditional tea heritage. Beyond Anxi Tieguanyin, Yongchun Foshou and Nan'an Shiting Green teas each have distinctive characters, testifying to the richness of Chinese tea culture.

Heritage Meets the Present · The Rebirth of Tradition

传统不是过去式，而是进行时

Tradition Is Not Past Tense — It Is Present Continuous

非遗的生命力在于传承，更在于创新。在泉州，古老的非遗正在以年轻人喜欢的方式回归生活。

The vitality of intangible heritage lies in transmission—and innovation. In Quanzhou, ancient heritage is returning to daily life in ways young people love.

案例一 Case 1 · Cultural IP

蟳埔女的“出圈”

Xunpu Goes Viral

2023年，蟳埔女簪花围在社交媒体走红，全国各地游客专程到泉州体验簪花。原本只是渔村日常的头饰，变成了文化IP。当地村民成立了簪花体验合作社，年轻的"蟳埔女"们用短视频记录生活，让千年习俗在当代焕发新生。

In 2023, Xunpu's floral headdresses went viral on social media, drawing tourists nationwide. What was once daily village attire became a cultural IP. Local villagers founded a floral-headdress experience cooperative, and young Xunpu women document their lives on short video—giving a millennium-old custom new life.

案例二 Case 2 · Musical Innovation

南音的“新声”

Nanyin's New Voice

泉州的年轻音乐人将南音与流行音乐、电子乐结合，创作出"新南音"作品。南音传承人走进校园开设课程，让00后学生学习古乐。在泉州的livehouse里，南音与摇滚乐同台演出，古老的旋律找到了新的听众。

Young Quanzhou musicians fuse Nanyin with pop and electronic music to create "New Nanyin." Nanyin inheritors teach in schools, introducing post-2000s students to ancient music. In Quanzhou's livehouses, Nanyin shares the stage with rock—ancient melodies finding new audiences.

案例三  Case 3 · Global Design

德化白瓷的“出海”

Dehua Porcelain Goes Global

今天的德化白瓷不再只是传统佛像和花瓶，年轻设计师将白瓷与现代生活美学结合，设计出茶具、香器、首饰等文创产品，通过跨境电商销往全球。千年的"中国白"，以新的形态继续走向世界。

Today's Dehua porcelain is no longer just traditional Buddha statues and vases. Young designers combine white porcelain with modern lifestyle aesthetics, creating tea ware, incense holders, and jewelry sold globally via cross-border e-commerce. The millennium-old "China White" continues to reach the world in new forms.
