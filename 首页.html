一座中国城市的四重叙事

泉州，古称“刺桐”。宋元时期，它是"东方第一大港"，是海上丝绸之路的起点；今天，它是世界文化遗产城市，是民营经济的重镇，是两岸同胞血脉相连的前沿。这座城市，用四重身份，讲述着一个立体而真实的中国故事。

A Four-Fold Narrative of a Chinese City

Quanzhou, historically known as "Zayton," was the "Greatest Port of the East" during the Song and Yuan dynasties and a starting point of the Maritime Silk Road. Today, it is a UNESCO World Heritage City, a powerhouse of private enterprise, and a frontier of cross-strait kinship. Through four identities, this city tells a multidimensional and authentic story of China.
