Industrial Journey · From Grassroots to Global

泉州工业的四十年

Forty Years of Quanzhou Industry

萌芽起步：草根工业的诞生

Emergence: Birth of Grassroots Industry

1978 — 1991

改革开放初期，泉州人多地少、资源匮乏，国家投资少。面对困境，泉州人选择了"草根工业"道路——以家庭作坊、联户企业为起点，从"三来一补"起步。晋江陈埭镇的农民率先办起制鞋作坊，石狮出现了服装地摊市场。地方政府采取"宽松放任"的态度，不争论、不压制，为民间创业留出空间。

In the early reform era, Quanzhou had limited land, resources, and state investment. Facing this, Quanzhou people chose a "grassroots industry" path—starting with family workshops and joint household enterprises through "three supplies and one compensation." Farmers in Jinjiang's Chendai town pioneered shoe workshops, while Shishi saw clothing street markets emerge. Local governments took a hands-off approach, leaving room for private entrepreneurship.

关键数据 Key Data 1985年乡镇企业突破1万家 10,000+ township enterprises by 1985

规模扩张：从作坊到工厂

Scale Expansion: From Workshops to Factories

1992 — 2000

邓小平南方谈话后，泉州民营经济进入快速扩张期。家庭作坊升级为工厂，企业开始注重质量和品牌。1995年，晋江提出"质量立市"战略，政府开始从"放任"转向"引导"——设立质量检测中心、举办鞋业博览会、打击假冒伪劣。与此同时，企业之间的市场竞争日趋激烈，倒逼企业提升技术和管理。安踏、特步等品牌在这一时期创立，泉州鞋业从"贴牌代工"向"自主品牌"转型。

After Deng Xiaoping's Southern Tour, Quanzhou's private economy entered rapid expansion. Family workshops upgraded to factories, and enterprises began focusing on quality and branding. In 1995, Jinjiang launched a "Quality City" strategy—government shifted from hands-off to guidance, establishing quality testing centers, hosting shoe expos, and cracking down on counterfeits. Brands like Anta and Xtep were founded, as Quanzhou's shoe industry shifted from OEM to own brands.

关键数据 Key Data 1999年晋江被授予"中国鞋都" Jinjiang named "China's Shoe Capital" in 1999

品牌崛起：从国内到全球

Brand Rise: From Domestic to Global

2001 — 2012

加入WTO后，泉州企业迎来全球化机遇。政府推动"品牌之都"建设，出台品牌奖励政策，鼓励企业上市融资。2007年，安踏在香港上市，成为泉州体育用品行业第一家上市公司。随后，特步、361度、匹克等企业相继上市，形成了独特的"晋江体育用品板块"。企业从国内市场走向国际市场，安踏收购FILA中国业务，开启多品牌战略。

After China's WTO accession, Quanzhou enterprises seized globalization opportunities. The government promoted a "Brand Capital" initiative with brand rewards and IPO support. In 2007, Anta listed in Hong Kong—the first IPO in Quanzhou's sports goods industry. Xtep, 361°, and Peak followed, forming a unique "Jinjiang sports goods cluster." Anta acquired FILA China, launching a multi-brand strategy.

关键数据 Key Data 2012年GDP突破5000亿，上市公司超70家 GDP exceeded 500B RMB, 70+ listed companies in 2012

创新升级：从制造到智造

Innovation Upgrade: From Manufacturing to Smart Making

2013 — Present

面对劳动力成本上升和产业转型压力，泉州推动"中国制造2025"试点，建设智能制造示范工厂。政府引导企业加大研发投入，建设工业设计中心、产业技术研究院。安踏成立运动科学实验室，研发投入占比超过5%；九牧建设智能卫浴工厂，实现自动化生产。同时，泉州企业加速"出海"，在东南亚、非洲建设生产基地，通过"21世纪海上丝绸之路"拓展全球市场。

Facing rising labor costs and transformation pressure, Quanzhou became a "Made in China 2025" pilot city, building smart manufacturing factories. Government guided enterprises to increase R&D. Anta's sports science lab invests over 5% of revenue in R&D; Jomoo built smart bathroom factories with automated production. Meanwhile, Quanzhou enterprises accelerated "going global," building production bases in Southeast Asia and Africa via the "21st Century Maritime Silk Road."

晋江经验——中国民营企业的领航

The Jinjiang Experience: Code of China's County Economy

2002年，时任福建省省长的习近平同志总结“晋江经验”，指出晋江的发展道路是中国特色社会主义市场经济的成功实践。其核心是：始终坚持以发展社会生产力为根本方向，始终坚持以市场为导向发展经济，始终坚持在顽强拼搏中取胜，始终坚持以诚信促进市场经济健康发展，始终坚持立足本地优势加快发展，始终坚持加强政府对市场经济的引导和服务。

In 2002, then-Fujian Governor Xi Jinping summarized the "Jinjiang Experience," noting that Jinjiang's development path was a successful practice of a socialist market economy with Chinese characteristics. Its core: always prioritizing productive forces, always taking a market-oriented approach, always winning through tenacity, always promoting healthy market development through integrity, always leveraging local advantages, and always strengthening government guidance and services.

故事一 · 体育用品 Story 1 · Sports Goods

丁世忠：从摆地摊到全球第三

Ding Shizhong: From Street Vendor to Global #3

ANTA · 安踏体育

1987年，17岁的丁世忠带着600双晋江产的运动鞋，只身来到北京推销。他摆过地摊，跑过商场，用了两年时间把晋江鞋卖进了北京的大商场。1991年，他回到晋江创办安踏。三十多年后，安踏成为全球第三大运动品牌，市值超千亿。

In 1987, 17-year-old Ding Shizhong took 600 pairs of Jinjiang-made sports shoes to Beijing alone. He sold on streets and pitched department stores, taking two years to get Jinjiang shoes into Beijing's major retailers. In 1991, he returned to found Anta. Over 30 years later, Anta is the world's third-largest sports brand.

“晋江人的特点就是爱拼才会赢，不做就什么都没有。”
"Jinjiang people's trait is winning through hard work. Do nothing, and you have nothing."
故事二 · 智能卫浴 Story 2 · Smart Bathroom

林孝发：从水龙头到智能卫浴

Lin Xiaofa: From Faucets to Smart Bathrooms

JOMOO · 九牧厨卫

1990年，林孝发在南安创办九牧，从生产最简单的水龙头开始。他坚持自主研发，从传统卫浴到智能马桶，再到整体卫浴解决方案，九牧一步步成为中国卫浴行业领军品牌。今天，九牧的智能工厂实现了生产自动化，产品销往全球数十个国家。

In 1990, Lin Xiaofa founded Jomoo in Nan'an, starting with the simplest faucets. Insisting on independent R&D, Jomoo progressed from traditional bathroom fixtures to smart toilets to total bathroom solutions, becoming China's leading bathroom brand. Today, Jomoo's smart factory achieves automated production, with products sold in dozens of countries.

“做企业就要敢为人先，别人不敢做的我们来做。”
"In business, you must dare to be first—what others won't do, we will."
故事三 · 休闲食品 Story 3 · Snack Food

许世辉：从小食品厂到休闲食品帝国

Xu Shihui: From Small Factory to Snack Empire

DALI · 达利食品

1989年，许世辉在惠安创办达利食品，从生产饼干开始。他敏锐捕捉市场需求，先后推出"达利园"蛋黄派、"好吃点"饼干、"和其正"凉茶等爆款产品。今天，达利集团是中国最大的休闲食品企业之一，产品覆盖全国，远销海外。

In 1989, Xu Shihui founded Dali Foods in Hui'an, starting with biscuits. Sensing market demand, he launched hit products like Daliyuan egg yolk pies, Haochidian cookies, and Heqizheng herbal tea. Today, Dali Group is one of China's largest snack food companies, with products nationwide and exported abroad.
